Year two. Your friend Marco wants to open a restaurant. He's good. He's passionate. He needs two hundred thousand dollars.

And you really want to say yes.

But back in week one, your planner made you set up a fun-money bucket: five percent of the winnings. That's the money for risky ideas, toys, and friends' dreams. When it's empty, it's empty.

So you offer Marco fifty thousand from the bucket. Not two hundred.

Eighteen months later, the restaurant closes. You're sad for Marco. But you're not ruined.
