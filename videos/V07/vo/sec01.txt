Day zero. It's a Wednesday. You bought the ticket on the way home, because the jackpot hit twenty million dollars and everyone at work was talking about it.

First number. Match. Second. Match.

Third. Fourth. Fifth.

The last ball.

Match!

Two tickets matched that night. The twenty million is split down the middle. Your half: ten million dollars.

And here's the first moment that decides how this ends. You want to call your mom. Your best friend. Your group chat. Your boss, so you can quit.

You don't call anyone.

Because the second people know, your life stops being yours. You haven't got a plan yet, so for now, the only plan is silence.
