Week one. You still haven't claimed the prize. Most lotteries give you months, not days, so there's time to do it properly.

First, you hire three people: a fee-only financial planner, who's paid by you and not by commissions on what they sell you; a tax accountant; and a lawyer.

You interview several of each. You ask how they get paid. And you notice which ones get excited about your money, and which ones get excited about a plan.

Then the lawyer tells you something you didn't know. In about a dozen states, jackpot winners can stay completely anonymous. In others, your name and your town can become public record, and some states only offer privacy above a certain prize size.

You live in a state where you can claim quietly, so you do.
