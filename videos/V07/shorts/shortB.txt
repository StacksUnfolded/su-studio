Seventy percent of lottery winners go broke. You've heard that, right?

The group it's usually credited to says it isn't their research, and they can't confirm it.

But here's the real study. About thirty-five thousand Florida lottery winners. People who won up to a hundred and fifty thousand dollars avoided bankruptcy at first. Three to five years later? The money had mostly just delayed it.

Because a windfall doesn't fix money habits. It just feeds them. And that's why…
