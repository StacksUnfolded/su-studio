# Stacks Unfolded V07: ChatGPT image prompts

**Video:** POV: You Win $10 Million and Have 5 Years Not to Go Broke (script v1)
**Use with:** the same ChatGPT "Stacks Unfolded" Project (H sheet, cast sheet and style instructions already loaded).
**Save to:** `G:\Youtube\Automation\Stacks Unfolded\V07\frames\` with the filename shown. ChatGPT's own names don't matter: I match each image to its shot from a contact sheet.

## How to run this list

1. **Do Part 0 first** (Dez and the new cast, then props). Dez appears in nine shots, so get him right before anything else, and paste his sheet into every Dez chat.
2. **One ChatGPT chat per location.** Generate the MASTER, reroll until he's right, then run the PLATE and the EDITS in that same chat.
3. **PLATE = the same room with the host removed.** That lets me animate him with library poses. Plates are only listed where I need them.
4. **No text anywhere.** Not on the ticket, the giant check, the FOR SALE sign, screens, envelopes or the calendar. Every word and number is added in the edit.
5. **No logos and no real people.** No real lottery, bank, car or crypto brand. The TV shows plain coloured balls.

**Already in the library, no need to generate:** the office cubicle (desk scenes), the home office (tax bill, watch on the laptop), the bank interior, the boardroom, the accountant (C06), host poses P01–P24 and props such as cash, moneybags, wallet, coffee, piggybank and bills.

**Totals:** 1 cast sheet, 1 prop sheet, 11 masters and new scenes, 5 plates and 13 edits. That's 31 images.

## Checklist for each image before saving

- The host matches the H sheet: hood up, sunglasses with no eyes drawn on the lenses, grey hoodie, never baby-like.
- Dez looks the same in every shot: backwards red cap, gold chain, red-and-black varsity jacket, no sunglasses.
- Hands touch what they hold.
- No text anywhere, especially on the ticket, the giant check, the sign, envelopes, screens and the menu.
- Plates are really empty: no leftover hands, shadows or ghost outlines.
- Keep faces and key objects away from the top 30% of the frame where possible, so on-screen numbers have room.
