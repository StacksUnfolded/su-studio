Level seven. Three thousand dollars a month. On the side.

So naturally… you reward yourself.

Newer car. Better phone. Nicer apartment. More takeout, because who has time to cook when you're running an empire?

And then one day, you notice something scary. Your side hustle isn't extra money anymore. It's holding up your whole life.

This is lifestyle creep. And it's sneaky, because every single upgrade felt reasonable. But now you can't have a slow month. You can't take a break. The hustle owns you.

The people who avoid it treat hustle money as a different kind of money. It goes into the business, into savings, into paying off debt. Anything except quietly raising the cost of normal life.

Because in two levels, you're going to want the freedom to make a very big decision. And lifestyle creep is how people lose that freedom… without even noticing.
