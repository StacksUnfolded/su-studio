Look at this checkout.

The pay later button is bigger than the pay now button. There's a countdown timer. "Only three left." And the big number is fifteen dollars… the real price, sixty, is in tiny letters underneath.

None of that is an accident. Stores pay these apps a fee, because people who can split a price buy more, and buy bigger.

And it works. Americans spent twenty billion dollars online with buy now, pay later in the twenty twenty-five holiday season alone.

So before you tap that button, ask one thing: could I pay the full price today?

If not… look at this checkout again.
