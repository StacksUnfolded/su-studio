Level eight. Paying debt with debt.

So you get creative.

Payment due on app one? Put it on your credit card. Groceries again? New plan on app three. Rent short this month? Borrow from Jay. Pay Jay back with… another plan.

This is the moment the zero percent loan quietly becomes a very expensive one. Because the credit card you just used to cover a "Free" Payment? That one does charge interest. And a lot of it, every month you don't clear it.

Remember lifestyle creep from the side hustle video? Each upgrade felt reasonable, until your life depended on money you didn't really have. This is the same trap, just faster. And with more apps.
